# Problem statement

Build an internal tool that lets staff export customer records. It must follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and it must call our status service before each export.

- DPDP Act, MeitY PDF: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf

- NIST AI RMF, publication page: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
